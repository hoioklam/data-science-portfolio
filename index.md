## 1. Introduction & Research Scope

Higher education is generally regarded as an important pathway for improving employment opportunities and increasing economic returns. However, with the expansion of higher education and the transformation of Macao's economic structure, it is important to examine whether higher education continues to provide substantial advantages in the labour market.

### Overarching Research Question
> **"Did the labour-market value of higher education in Macao show proportional growth from 2014 to 2024?"**

### Project Sub-Questions
To address this central question, the project investigates the following specific areas:
1. How have the number and proportion of highly educated workers in Macao changed over the period from 2014 to 2024?
2. To what extent has the expansion of higher education been associated with improvements in employment outcomes?
3. How have employment and income trends varied across different industries in Macao?
4. How have major economic events, such as the COVID-19 pandemic, affected employment and income trends in Macao?

---

## 2. Project Tasks & Methodology

The workflow is divided into four main tasks implemented using Python in Jupyter Notebooks:

* **Task 1: Data Collection** — Gathering official datasets from DSEC and DSEDJ covering employment statistics, unemployment statistics, income indicators, CPI data, education statistics, and higher education statistics.
* **Task 2: Data Cleaning and Preparation** — Processing datasets using Python in Jupyter Notebook, handling missing values, checking inconsistent formats, standardizing variables, and preparing data for analysis.
* **Task 3: Feature Construction and Analysis** — Constructing indicators such as higher education employment ratio, graduate growth rate, unemployment indicators, and inflation-adjusted real income, followed by statistical analysis.
* **Task 4: Data Visualization** — Creating time-series charts, comparison charts, scatter plots, and heatmaps to explain changes in education supply, employment outcomes, and income returns.
